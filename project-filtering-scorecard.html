Team sync (share with your team without Claude)

One person does steps 1 to 4 once. Everyone else just opens the invite link. Until then, the page works alone on this device.

  1. Open console.firebase.google.com, create a project (Analytics not needed).
  2. Build, then Realtime Database, then Create database.
  3. Open the Rules tab and paste: {"rules":{"board":{".read":true,".write":true}}} then Publish.
  4. Copy the database URL (it ends in firebaseio.com or firebasedatabase.app) and paste it below.

Anyone with the link can read and write the board, so keep private information out of it. No server option: export your data, send the file, and import teammates’ files.
